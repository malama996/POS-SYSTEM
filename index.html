📱 How to Install ShopPOS on Phone

Install ShopPOS as a native app on your home screen to work 100% offline without opening an app store.

On Android (Chrome / Edge)
1
Tap the Browser Menu (3 dots ⋮) in top right corner.
2
Select "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App".
On iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1
Tap the Share Button (square with arrow ↑) at the bottom of Safari.
2
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
